Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How soon can you schedule an inspection?

A: Most inspections are available within 24–48 hours, depending on the location and time of year.

Q: How long does an inspection take?

A: A typical home inspection takes 3–4 hours, depending on size and condition.

Q: Do I need to be there?

A: You're welcome to attend, especially for the walkthrough at the end, but it’s not required.

Q: When will I get the report?

A: Reports are delivered the same day or within 24 hours.

Q: What does the report include?

A: A visual, photo-based breakdown of systems, issues, and recommendations.

Q: Do you work with realtors?

A: Yes — I regularly coordinate with agents to keep the transaction moving smoothly.
